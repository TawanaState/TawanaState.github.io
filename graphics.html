Illumination Models

Gouraud Shading: Calculates lighting at the vertices. Colors are interpolated across the face.

Phong Shading: Calculates lighting at every pixel. Normals are interpolated.

How to see Gouraud fail:

1. Switch Shading to "Gouraud".
2. Lower the "Resolution" to 8.
3. Increase "Shininess".
Notice how the bright specular highlight warps or completely disappears because the vertices miss the light!
